# Product Guidelines: Archetype

## Prose & Tone
- **Empathetic & Objective**: Use language that feels personal but remains scientifically grounded. Avoid jargon where possible, but don't oversimplify complex psychological concepts.
- **Encouraging**: During the onboarding and quiz phases, use micro-copy that encourages users to be honest and patient.
- **Clarity**: Results and archetypes should be described clearly, highlighting both strengths and areas for growth without being judgmental.

## Design & UX
- **Focus on Flow**: The quiz experience should be distraction-free, with smooth transitions between questions.
- **Visual Feedback**: Use subtle animations (via Reanimated) to provide feedback for user actions (e.g., selecting an answer, completing a phase).
- **Dark Mode First**: Given the "Archetype" branding, the UI should prioritize a sophisticated dark aesthetic, using vibrant accent colors for different personality facets.
- **Privacy by Design**: Users should always feel in control of their data. Privacy settings should be easy to find and understand.

## Scientific Integrity
- **Model Adherence**: Always reference the IPIP-NEO model correctly. Ensure that facets and domains are represented accurately in visualizations.
- **Contextual Help**: Provide tooltips or "learn more" links for psychological terms and archetype descriptions.

## Community & Interaction
- **Positive Discovery**: The feed should promote constructive interactions. Use "Serendipity" to frame differing viewpoints as opportunities for learning rather than conflict.
- **Authenticity**: Badges and archetypes are earned through the quiz, maintaining the integrity of the community's psychometric makeup.
