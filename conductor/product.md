# Initial Concept

A psychometric social media platform where personality is the primary filter for community interaction. Built with React Native (Expo) and FastAPI.

# Product Guide: Archetype

## Vision
To create a social media experience that prioritizes meaningful human connections through psychometric alignment. By using the scientifically-validated IPIP-NEO Big Five personality model, Archetype transforms community interaction from generic algorithms to personality-driven discovery.

## Target Audience
- Psychometrics Enthusiasts: Users interested in deep self-discovery.
- Community Builders: People seeking niche groups with shared temperaments.
- Social Explorers: Users looking for perspectives that challenge or complement their own.

## Core Pillars
1. **Scientific Integrity**: Grounded in the IPIP-NEO model with rigorous scoring engines.
2. **Interactive Visualization**: Transforming dry data into dynamic, beautiful radar charts and badges.
3. **Personality-Filtered Feed**: A feed that can be toggled between "Similar Minds" and "Serendipity" modes.
4. **Onboarding as Experience**: A two-phase progressive quiz that balances ease-of-entry with depth-of-insight.

## Functional Requirements
- **Psychometric Engine**: Integration with IPIP-NEO 120/300 question banks and population norms.
- **Mobile Experience**: A cross-platform mobile app with high-performance animations and smooth navigation.
- **Community Interaction**: A feed with psychometric ranking and profile badges representing one of 12 archetypes.
- **Security & Privacy**: Secure JWT authentication and robust protection against IDOR (Insecure Direct Object Reference).

## Success Metrics
- Conversion rate from Phase 1 to Phase 2 quiz completion.
- User engagement within psychometrically-filtered feeds.
- Accuracy and resonance of assigned archetypes as reported by users.
